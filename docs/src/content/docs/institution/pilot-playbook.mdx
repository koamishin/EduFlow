The pilot product question is: **what should the institution do with the fees it has
already collected from students?** OpenSinop reviews those funds alongside approved
budgets and commitments, proposes which approved obligations can be funded, and explains
any holds or shortfalls. Finance staff keep every decision.

The college keeps collecting fees and paying bills through its existing local-currency
process. Pilot planning uses approved opening funds or staff-verified aggregate realized
receipts — it needs no student records, a new fee portal, SIS or bank integration.

## Minimum inputs (supplied or confirmed by authorised staff)

- **Department and budget period** — approved allocation, already-spent/committed
  amounts, restricted funds and protected reserves. Allocation is not proof of cash.
- **Staff-verified aggregate realized receipts** or approved opening funds — source
  stream/reference, non-overlapping collection interval, source-document digest, exact
  original currency/amount, restrictions, and independent review. Opening funds must
  exclude the same receipts. Expected tuition, promised grants and forecast revenue are
  **not** available funds.
- **Anonymised approved bills** — stable source reference, original amount/currency, due
  date, budget category, vendor alias, business approval, and recurring-obligation
  evidence where relevant.
- **Current approved finance policy** — authorised reviewers, exact mirror-rate/rounding
  rule, allow-listed controlled testnet recipients, spend/fee caps, and a stop/recovery
  owner.

## The workflow

<Steps>

1. **Capture and review collections.** Cashier/finance staff record or import aggregate
   receipts (`CollectionBatch`); a separate verified admin reviews each batch. Only
   reviewed, unrestricted receipts can fund a plan.

2. **Capture bill evidence and destinations.** A maker records each bill's exact source
   amount/currency with source and business-approval references; a separate checker
   approves the evidence and the vendor's testnet destination with independent control
   verification. Destination approval is never payment approval.

3. **Snapshot the budget and plan.** Staff capture a `BudgetSnapshot` binding allocation,
   spend, commitments, reviewed receipts, opening funds, outflows, restricted cash and
   protected reserve; the deterministic planner walks that closed bill set and proposes
   payment order with reasons.

4. **Review, prepare, authorise.** A supervisor accepts the plan; the maker prepares an
   immutable payment intent (`can_execute=false`); a funding window admits bounded
   capacity holds; an **enrolled** reviewer authorises the payment with fresh
   password + TOTP within 5 minutes. Payment authorisation and plan acceptance are
   different acts held by different people.

5. **Submit and verify.** With the submission runtime explicitly enabled (testnet
   only), the isolated executor submits each authorised payment, observes the Circle
   transaction id to a hash, and `ArcSettlementVerifier` records a matching successful
   receipt with finality. Unknown outcomes stay unresolved; retries cannot pay twice.

</Steps>

### Two execution lanes

- **Human path (default).** Each eligible admin-approved item produces its own linked
  USDC payment on `ARC-TESTNET` through the existing Circle Agent Wallet / Lepton path.
  An unrelated transfer, fake receipt or read-only dashboard does not complete it.
- **Capped autonomous lane (separately authorised, testnet-only).** Once the human path
  passes its gates, a reviewed standing mandate may let existing approved recurring bills
  from allow-listed vendors run automatically, within per-payment and cumulative limits,
  budget, reserve and fee protection. Everything else goes to staff; hard policy failures
  stay held. The autonomous allowance starts at zero.

## Money and privacy boundaries

- Preserve the exact local amount/currency and a staff-approved immutable reference-rate
  snapshot with rounding to six-decimal USDC units. Testnet mapping is not real FX or bank
  settlement; never assume one local-currency unit equals one USDC.
- Use approved, controlled testnet recipients linked off-chain to vendor aliases. A
  mirror does not mean the real vendor received money or accepts USDC. Keep local
  actual-paid state separate from testnet mirror state; never clear the college's payable
  from testnet proof.
- Use verified faucet-funded balances with fee allowance. If funds cannot cover
  full-value mirrors, select smaller real bills or obtain sufficient authorised testnet
  funding — never silently scale source amounts or seed fictional funds.
- Keep identities and sensitive documents off-chain. Get separate permission for
  external AI processing, testnet execution, staff recording and any public college
  name/logo/quotes.

## Money posture: the three money flows

| Flow | Direction | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student tuition | student → college | Student consents to a bounded transfer; posting derives from verified evidence. Testnet USDC is **not** official tuition — it is a designated-account allocation only |
| Student bill payment | student → college | Inbound lane: records a classification assessment only (`approved_amount = 0`, requires human approval); it never carries spend authority |
| Vendor bill payment | institution → vendor | Full maker/checker lifecycle: policy, reservation, MFA authorisation, executor, settlement verification |

## Evidence to collect for a pilot

- Video of staff using the workflow (college-authorised), linked reasoning/audit records,
  and verified testnet transaction URLs.
- Proposals approved as-is versus edited/rejected, holds/pending separately, review time
  against an agreed baseline including correction effort, and automatic versus escalated
  outcomes.
- Staff agreement with automatic decisions counted **separately** from per-item approvals.
- Sample sizes, feedback and limitations — no invented savings, adoption or guaranteed
  promotion.

## Operating the pilot safely

- Keep the stop switch ready: `EDUFLOW_*_STOP` halts new submissions while leaving
  reconciliation and recovery running.
- Run supervised durable queue workers plus the scheduler, with event/outbox recovery,
  deduplicated occurrences and bounded retries.
- Reading a notification is not approval; silence never authorises payment. Pause new
  submissions or suspend a mandate while preserving in-flight reconciliation and audit.
- Compare verified testnet transaction evidence with each authorised bill, while keeping
  actual local collections/payments in the school's existing accounting process.
