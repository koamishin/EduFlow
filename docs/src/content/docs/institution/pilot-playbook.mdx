---
title: Run a small pilot
description: Evaluate one department's collection and planning workflow without turning a trial into real money movement.
---

The first pilot should answer one practical question: **Does OpenSinop help staff
review received funds and approved bills more clearly than their current process?**

Start with planning, not an automated payment demonstration.

## Agree on a small scope

Choose one department, one budget period, and a few already approved bills. Keep the
school's real collection and payment processes unchanged.

Have the finance owner confirm:

- The source reports you may use and whether they must be anonymized.
- The approved allocation, amounts already spent, commitments, restrictions, and reserve.
- Who records evidence and who checks it independently.
- Who investigates errors and decides when to stop the pilot.
- Whether external AI processing is permitted. It is optional, not a prerequisite.

Do not publish a school name, logo, recording, or quotation without separate permission.

## Prepare the instance

Ask IT for a dedicated evaluation instance, not a copy with live credentials.
Use the fake driver, disable the automatic bridge, and do not configure live signing
for the first trial. Confirm the runtime settings with the operator, not only the UI.

See [Configure your instance](../../getting-started/configuration/) and
[Current limits](../safety-readiness/). Disabling bridge scheduling alone is not a
complete block on every bridge entry point.

## Walk through one example

1. **Capture a collection.** Use a permitted completed report with its original amount,
   currency, dates, and restrictions. Follow [Record and review collections](../../guides/collections/).
2. **Review it independently.** Have the reviewer explain what they checked. Keep pending
   or disputed receipts outside available cash.
3. **Select the bills.** Use existing approved obligations. The planner does not discover
   which expenses the school should incur.
4. **Capture and review the plan.** Check both budget and cash, not a combined balance.
   Follow [Plan department spending](../../guides/planning/).
5. **Record the staff decision.** Accept, reject, or request a correction, with the reason.
   Stop here for a planning-only pilot. No payment is needed to prove this part useful.

## Compare with the usual process

Record a small, honest set of observations:

- Could staff find the source behind each amount?
- Did reviewers understand why a bill was proposed or held?
- Which proposals were accepted unchanged, corrected, rejected, or still unresolved?
- How long did review and correction take compared with the agreed baseline?
- Which setup or screen problems needed operator help?

Report the sample size and limitations. Do not count a held bill as a successful
payment or invent time savings from a single demonstration.

## If you later add testnet payments

Treat this as a separately approved phase. Require controlled recipients, actual
testnet funding, exact source-to-testnet mapping, independent payment review, a recovery
owner, and evidence of the matching transfer.

Keep the local bill and real payment records separate. The ordinary reviewed path has
tests using mocked provider and chain evidence; that is not proof of a live institutional
rollout. The automatic bridge has materially different behavior and should not be used
to demonstrate independent human authorization.

## Decide whether to expand

The finance owner should review the evidence and unresolved problems. Add departments
or integrations only after the initial workflow is understandable and useful. A working
demo does not authorize mainnet payments, student custody, or use of real school funds.
