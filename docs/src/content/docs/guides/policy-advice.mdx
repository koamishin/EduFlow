title: Use policy advice
description: Understand institution policies, advisory evaluations, and what a policy match does—and does not—approve.
---

OpenSinop has optional tools to explain how a collection or proposed expense relates to
your institution's written policy. Advice helps a reviewer; it does not replace one.

## Two kinds of policy

**Institution policy** contains the school's clauses, categories, examples, and
clarifications. Staff use it to ask how a source should be classified.

**Finance controls** contain exact limits such as minimum reserve, daily disbursement
limit, automatic payment limit, and maximum fee. The application checks these in code.

Publishing an institution policy can activate its linked finance controls. Publication
is a meaningful configuration decision, not just saving text, but it is still not
approval of an individual payment.

## Prepare and publish a policy

In **Institution policies**, authorized staff save a revision with clauses, categories,
examples, and exact controls. The normal publication path requires analysis of the
saved revision, passing saved examples, resolved clarification questions, and an
independent authorized reviewer.

If the active controls change separately, the old publication is not automatically
treated as current advice. Refresh the context before requesting another evaluation.

## Request advice about a source

Open **Policy evaluations**, or choose **Re-evaluate policy** from a collection.
The application can evaluate recorded collections, transactions, or eligible USDC
budget snapshots against the active policy.

You need an active policy, a configured advisory provider when required by the chosen
engine, and a durable queue. An evaluation request does not review the receipt, reserve
funds, or submit a payment.

## Read the result

- **Match:** a suggested category or clause fit. It is not payment approval.
- **Needs review:** uncertainty or missing evidence needs attention.
- **Hold:** inspect the recorded reasons. Do not assume every hold is an enforced transfer stop.
- **Exempt:** the evaluation treated the source as excluded under its rules, not exempt from every financial control.

The dashboard's confidence, concurrence, and timing figures describe recorded
evaluations and reviews. They do not certify accuracy, savings, or financial safety.

:::caution[Receipt approval is a separate action]
The Policy evaluations screen also has a receipt approval action. For tuition-linked
collections, that action can invoke the experimental automatic bridge when enabled.
Reading advice is not the same as clicking approval. See
[Current limits](../../institution/safety-readiness/#experimental-automatic-bridge).
:::

## If advice is unavailable

Keep using the source evidence and ordinary review process. Do not invent a policy
match. Ask your operator to check provider settings, the active policy, queue readiness,
and the evaluation's error or missing-evidence message.
