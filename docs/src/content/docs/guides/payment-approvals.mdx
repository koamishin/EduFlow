---
title: Review a payment
description: Check an exact reserved payment, understand reviewer requirements, and distinguish approval from provider acceptance and settlement.
---

Use **Payment Approvals** for the ordinary reviewed vendor payment path. It is separate
from receipt review, policy advice, and plan acceptance.

**Result:** an approval, rejection, or hold on an exact reserved payment. A network
approval can create submission work for an enabled worker. Recording it is not proof
that the transfer has settled.

## Check that you can review

The screen shows reviewer readiness. The normal path requires:

- Verified email and a finance/admin role.
- Directly assigned `AuthorizePayment:PaymentIntent` permission, not only a role label.
- An authenticator independently enrolled for payment review by a different authorized admin.
- A reviewer different from the people who prepared the intent and reserved capacity.

Changing or disabling the enrolled authenticator invalidates eligibility. Ask your
operator about enrollment; sharing a code or signing in as the preparer is not a workaround.

## Review the exact payment

1. Open the reserved bill and choose **View evidence**.
2. Check the bill reference, treasury wallet, full recipient address, network, exact
   USDC amount, and fee ceiling. Verify the destination against your approved source,
   not merely a familiar abbreviated address.
3. Check the current funding window and whether evidence is simulated or from the network.
4. Choose **Review payment**. Select approve, reject, or hold and explain the decision.
5. For approval, check the expiry. Supply your own fresh password and unused
   authenticator code, then choose **Record payment review**.

Approval lasts at most five minutes and cannot outlive its funding window. Material
changes require new reviewed evidence. Repeating an old request does not renew it.

## What happens next?

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Approval recorded** | Authority was recorded for the exact evidence. It is not a receipt. |
| **On hold / Rejected** | No approval was granted. Reserved capacity is not automatically released. |
| **Accepted by provider** | The provider has a request ID; a matching onchain transfer may not exist yet. |
| **Verified settlement** | The verifier matched successful movement to the expected payment. |
| **Unknown / Needs investigation** | Evidence is insufficient to conclude the outcome. Do not send again blindly. |

An enabled worker can process durable submission work after approval. The payment
screen does not call the provider itself. With the runtime disabled, an approval does
not by itself turn submission on.

## Testnet is not payment of the real bill

The reviewed payment path is limited to `ARC-TESTNET` (chain ID `5042002`). Keep the
institution's real local payment status in its existing accounting process. A verified
test transfer to a pilot address does not establish that the real supplier was paid.

The experimental bridge is an exception: it can generate approval inputs and mark an
invoice paid after testnet verification. It is not this independent staff review process.
Read [Current limits](../../institution/safety-readiness/) before using it.

## If approval is refused

Read the reason: expired funding, changed source evidence, missing direct permission,
changed authenticator, and an already recorded decision are different problems. Do not
delete history, release a hold, or create a second payment simply to bypass the refusal.

## Next step

Track the existing submission in **Payment submissions** or **Payment progress**.
Ask your recovery owner to investigate unresolved outcomes using
[Troubleshooting](../../reference/troubleshooting/).
