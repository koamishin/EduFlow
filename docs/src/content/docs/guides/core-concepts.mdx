---
title: Money and evidence
description: How exact amounts, source currency, snapshots, and settlement matching should be interpreted in OpenSinop.
---

This is developer reference. For staff-facing explanations, start with
[How OpenSinop works](../workflows/) or [Terms in plain English](../../reference/glossary/).

## An amount always has a currency

New evidence paths use exact minor units. USDC has six decimal places; the supported
fiat codes use two. Serialize large minor-unit amounts as strings when crossing JSON
boundaries, not floating-point chart values.

`Money` and `CurrencyValuationCalculator` handle the application's exact representation.
Lepton's `Amounts` helpers handle package conversions. Older treasury/payment APIs and
two-decimal monetary storage remain; do not claim the whole ledger is already exact.

### Local amount, reference valuation, and settlement differ

Keep the original source amount and currency. A stored local-to-USDC reference rate
and rounding rule explain a valuation; they are not an executable FX offer or proof
that local cash became USDC.

`EDUFLOW_DISPLAY_CURRENCY` affects presentation. Changing it does not convert stored
records. Indicative fallback rates must not silently become payment authority.

## Budget and cash are independent checks

The department's allocation is not added to cash. Opening funds must exclude selected
receipts, cash protection buckets must not overlap, and other commitments must exclude
the selected bills when the snapshot says so.

`DepartmentBudgetPlanner` verifies bound evidence, orders the selected bill set, and
reduces budget and cash cumulatively. It emits proposals or holds, with `can_execute=false`.
It does not decide to incur new obligations or choose a new vendor.

## Snapshots and reviews

A snapshot binds relevant source fields and a canonical digest. A reviewer must act
against the exact expected content, not whatever a later page refresh happens to show.
Material changes need successor evidence rather than mutation of an approved intent.

A digest is change detection, not a digital signature, source authenticity certificate,
or proof that an independent human actually reviewed it. In particular, automatically
generated review records in the experimental bridge must not be described as a fresh
person's sign-off.

## Arc has one USDC balance, exposed two ways

Arc's native view uses 18 decimals; the ERC-20 USDC view uses six. They represent the
same pool of funds. Do not add both balances or treat them as separate assets.

Native quantities can exceed PHP's integer range. Use string/arbitrary-precision
conversion, not `hexdec()` followed by an integer cast. Display or chart conversions
must never become the authoritative stored amount.

## Fees depend on who pays

The current agent-wallet transport declares `RelayerSponsored`; its callers should not
automatically charge the payer the receipt's relayer gas cost. `SignerEnforced` and
`Unbounded` are different fee guarantees, not interchangeable labels.

The transport rejects a requested `max_fee_base_units` it cannot honor. Developer-wallet
execution remains blocked in this path. A fee estimate is not an enforceable ceiling.

## A hash is not settlement

Provider acceptance, transaction visibility, receipt success, and matching settlement
are separate facts. Dedicated verification must match successful movement to the
expected network, asset, economic sender, recipient, and exact amount.

Relayed transactions can have a different `tx.from` from the economic payer; the
verifier inspects supported transfer evidence rather than assuming the envelope sender
is the wallet. Unknown emitters or unreadable evidence should not be guessed.

The legacy reconciliation command's `verified` result is weaker than this complete
expected-payment match. Neither a null lookup nor provider timeout proves fabrication.

## Simulation is a separate outcome

Fake-driver results are rehearsals. They must remain visibly simulated and must not be
used as proof of network settlement or real institutional income. Testnet receipts are
also not official local-currency payment of a bill.

## AI is not authorization

Policy classifications and explanations do not grant spend authority. Review the
specific call path: an advisory envelope, a legacy assistance tool, and the automatic
bridge are different capabilities. Avoid a blanket claim that every financial action
is safe simply because the model does not sign transactions itself.
