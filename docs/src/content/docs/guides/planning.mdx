---
title: Plan department spending
description: Capture the department's budget and cash evidence, review the selected bills, and understand holds before preparing a payment.
---

Use **Finance Supervisor** to check whether a known set of approved bills fits the
department's remaining allocation and usable cash.

**Result:** a proposal with checks and remaining amounts for each bill. A proposal
does not reserve funds, approve a payment, or change a wallet balance.

## Before you start

You need an existing active department budget, reviewed bill evidence, and reviewed
collections or separately evidenced opening funds. Some source and funding setup uses
backend actions or operator commands; an empty screen is not permission to invent those records.

Prepare three things separately:

- **Budget:** approved allocation, amounts already spent, and other commitments.
- **Cash:** opening funds, reviewed received collections, and actual outflows.
- **Protection:** restricted cash, protected reserve, and other cash commitments.

## Budget is not cash

An approved allocation says what the department may spend. It does not prove that the
money has arrived. A bill must fit both remaining allocation and usable cash.

Likewise, opening funds must exclude the receipts you select for the snapshot.
Counting the same money in both fields makes a plan look funded when it is not.

## Capture a planning snapshot

1. Open **Finance Supervisor** and choose **Capture planning snapshot**.
2. Select the department budget, original currency, period, and evidence times.
3. Select the **Closed bill set**: the specific reviewed bill versions this plan should consider.
4. Select the approved receipt reviews and enter the exact budget, cash, and protection amounts.
5. Add source references and confirm that commitments and cash buckets do not overlap.

A snapshot is a recorded picture of the evidence at that time. It has an expiry.
Changed or expired evidence needs a new context, not a silent edit of an old approval.

## Read the proposal

Open **Details** for a background run. The planner orders the selected bills by due date
and ID, then checks approved evidence, remaining allocation, and remaining cash.

- **Propose human review:** this bill fits the checks in this plan. It is not approved for payment.
- **Hold:** one or more checks failed. Read the failed checks before changing any inputs.

The planner considers the bills cumulatively. An earlier eligible bill reduces the
capacity available for the next one. It does not discover new bills, rank arbitrary
expenses, or transfer money between departments.

## Review the plan

Use **Review proposal**, inspect the exact bill list and amounts, and choose:

- **Accept exact plan — no payment authority**
- **Reject plan**
- **Request correction**

Record a reason. Accepting a plan does not override missing evidence, expired funding,
or a blocked destination.

## Prepare a bill for payment review

For an accepted plan, **Prepare payment review** asks for an eligible proposed bill,
a separately reviewed funding window, and an exact maximum fee.

This holds capacity for the bill and fee. The hold is an application record, not a lock
on the provider wallet. It does not approve or submit the payment. A reviewer continues
in [Payment Approvals](../payment-approvals/).

## If nothing appears

Check that the snapshot was captured, its source records are reviewed, and the background
worker is running. **Queued** means work has not completed; **Waiting for review** means
a result needs staff attention. A **Completed** planning run is not a completed payment.

Operators can use [Run background services](../operations/) to investigate the worker.
