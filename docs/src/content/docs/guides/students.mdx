---
title: Student payment screens
description: Understand what students can view, what the pilot payment flow requires, and how testnet receipts differ from official tuition payments.
---

Students have screens for their own dashboard, accounts, payments, wallet, and assistance
features. These are separate from department budgeting and staff payment approval.

**The collection and planning workflow does not require students to use a wallet.**
A school can start with reviewed aggregate reports from its existing payment channels.

## What students can see

- **Dashboard:** account information and available student services.
- **My Payments / Pay screen:** tuition or campus bills, amounts, and existing payment attempts.
- **My Wallet:** the assigned pilot address and observed balance.
- **Financial Assistance:** a separate legacy assistance workflow, not fee collection approval.

The server identifies the student from the signed-in account and sets the institution
treasury destination. A student cannot select another student's account or redirect the
recipient through the normal tuition form.

## The wallet is a pilot wallet

Current generated student wallets are **operator-controlled** wallets attached to student
records for the pilot. They are not independently owned student wallets or a production
custody solution. Operators provision and fund them; wallet slots and faucet availability
are limited.

Do not invite students to send real money or treat this arrangement as consent to
institutional custody of their personal funds.

## What happens when a student tries a payment

1. The student requests a quote for an allowed tuition account or campus bill.
2. The quote binds an amount, payer, recipient, network, and expiry.
3. The student confirms that quote. Consent records an attempt; it is not settlement.
4. An enabled worker checks the configured rail, recovery owner, balance, and other
   conditions before submitting.
5. The application observes the provider request and verifies matching Arc movement
   before applying an eligible pilot receipt.

The live pilot is restricted to Arc Testnet and explicit account/bill allowlists.
Circle agent-wallet sponsorship and developer-wallet fee controls are different:
the current transport treats the agent-wallet rail as sponsored, while developer-wallet
execution remains blocked. A missing fee-cap API does not mean every agent-wallet transfer
is categorically impossible.

## Read the status before trying again

**Simulated / Rehearsal** means no network transfer happened. It must not create a real
receipt or clear a balance as though money arrived.

**Accepted / Awaiting verification** means the provider request is still being resolved.
**Unknown** requires investigation; do not confirm a second payment to “fix” it.

**Verified on Arc Testnet** means the pilot transfer was checked on the test network.
It is not an official school tuition receipt, even if a screen offers a receipt-style
document. Overpayments need separate handling and are not extra spendable income.

## For school staff

Use **Reconcile student payment** only for eligible existing movements, not to count
them again as a manual receipt. Tuition-linked collections may trigger the experimental
bridge; read [Current limits](../../institution/safety-readiness/) first.
