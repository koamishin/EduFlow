OpenSinop helps a school record money it has received, check department spending
against an approved budget, and keep the evidence behind each decision together.
It also has experimental payment workflows using Circle wallets and USDC on Arc Testnet.

**Your existing accounting system still matters.** Recording a receipt here does not
check your bank account. A testnet payment is not payment of a real school bill.

## Follow one collection through the application

1. **Record what arrived.** A finance officer enters a receipt or completed collection report.
2. **Check it independently.** Another authorized staff member checks the amount and any restrictions.
3. **Plan within the budget.** Staff select approved bills. OpenSinop checks whether both the allocation and available cash cover them.
4. **Keep payment decisions separate.** Accepting a plan is not the same as approving or verifying a payment.

For an example with ordinary school expenses, read [How OpenSinop works](guides/workflows/).
No student records or AI provider are needed for the aggregate collection and planning path.

## Before trying payments

OpenSinop is under development, not a certified school treasury system. Start with
simulated data. Treat network payments as a separately approved testnet experiment.

An experimental automatic bridge is **enabled by default** and has entry points in the
scheduler, command line, and staff screens. It is not a substitute for independent human
approval. Operators should read [Current limits](institution/safety-readiness/) before
connecting a wallet or starting background services.
